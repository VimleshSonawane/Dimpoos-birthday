# Poonam: Shinkai anime shot pack, slides 1 and 2

**How it works.** Make one image per shot, turn it into a moving clip, save the clip in the `clips/` folder with the exact file name below, and the site plays it automatically, in sync with the voice and subtitles. Any shot without a clip still shows the current drawn version, so the site never breaks while you work.

**Tools.** Images: Muse, ChatGPT image or Midjourney. Image to video: Kling, Runway or Luma. Export each clip as **MP4, 16:9, 1080p**. 10-second clips are best; 5-second clips work too, because the site slows a clip down (up to 2x) to fit its shot.

**Text stays in the site.** "Poonam", "the full moon", "Dimpoo", "February 1998" and "glug glug!" are added on top by the site, so leave all words out of the images. AI tools spell names badly.

> anime film still in the style of Makoto Shinkai, Comix Wave Films background art, hyper-detailed painted environment, luminous sky with glowing volumetric clouds, soft light rays, lens flare, rich saturated blues, violets, pinks and golds, warm rim light on characters, gentle film grain, cinematic 16:9 composition, no text, no watermark

Make these once, then reuse them as image references in later shots so everyone looks the same.

- **Poonam as a child:** upload her childhood photo and use "anime version of this girl, about 7 years old, long dark hair in a ponytail, big warm brown eyes, a dimple on her cheek, simple cotton frock".
- **Ajji:** "kind Indian grandmother in her sixties, grey hair in a bun, soft cotton saree with the pallu over her shoulder, gentle smile". Upload her photo if you have one.

- **Smita and Dushyant in 1998:** "young Indian couple in their twenties, 1990s clothing; she in a salwar kameez, he in a shirt". Show them from behind or in silhouette if you don't want to use their faces.
